# goip ↔ `ip` link kinds: implementation status

**Last updated:** 2026-10-25

**Summary:**
- The design and the task plan are in [goip-link-kinds.md](goip-link-kinds.md). This document tracks progress against them.
- The plan is written (DOC001).
- KC0 is done (KC0-T01): the capture drivers load the kind modules late, after every existing row, with no fallback tunnel devices in the namespaces made afterwards, and a `kinds` sidecar records what the guest kernel creates. It creates 31 of the 32 probed kinds. gtp is refused, and KC4 decides why.
- KC1 is done (KC1-T01): 18 `ip link set dev d0 inet …` rows, 11 the kernel answers and 7 `ip` refuses before netlink, are in the rtnl corpus. They are KP0's and KG0's source.
- KC2 is done (KC2-T01, KC2-T02): a kind corpus (`kind-write-corpus.exp`) the link-write driver runs into `link-write/kinds/`, with 60 bridge rows and 42 bridge_slave rows, 80 the kernel accepts, 12 it refuses and 10 `ip` refuses before netlink. They are KP1's and KG1's source.
- KC3 is done (KC3-T01, KC3-T02, KC3-T03): 28 vlan, 34 macvlan, 6 macvtap, 18 ipvlan and 4 ipvtap rows in the kind corpus, stacked on a dummy `d0`; of the 90, 54 the kernel accepts, 14 it refuses and 22 `ip` refuses before netlink. They are KP2's and KG2's source.
- KC4 is done (KC4-T01, KC4-T02, KC4-T03, KC4-T04): KC4-T01 adds 73 vxlan rows, 46 the kernel accepts, 12 it refuses and 15 `ip` refuses before netlink; KC4-T02 adds 49 geneve rows, 22 accepted, 13 refused by the kernel and 14 by `ip`; KC4-T03 adds 16 bareudp and 20 amt rows, 11 accepted, 12 refused by the kernel and 13 by `ip`; KC4-T04 adds 19 gtp rows, 7 accepted, 4 refused by the kernel and 8 by `ip`. They are KP3's and KG3's source.
- KC5 is done (KC5-T01, KC5-T02, KC5-T03, KC5-T04, KC5-T05; planned in DOC006): KC5-T01 adds 45 gre, 11 gretap and 25 erspan rows, 44 the kernel accepts, 16 it refuses and 21 `ip` refuses before netlink; KC5-T02 adds 36 ip6gre, 9 ip6gretap and 19 ip6erspan rows, 36 accepted, 14 refused by the kernel and 14 by `ip`; KC5-T03 adds 47 ipip and 37 sit rows, 46 accepted, 14 refused by the kernel and 24 by `ip` (`6rd-reset` has no row: it segfaults `ip`); KC5-T04 adds 49 ip6tnl rows, 29 accepted, 7 refused by the kernel and 13 by `ip`; KC5-T05 adds 23 vti, 12 vti6 and 23 xfrm rows, 27 accepted, 15 refused by the kernel and 16 by `ip`. They are KP4's and KG4's source.
- KC6 is in progress (planned in DOC007: four tasks, with 181 drafted rows for hsr, netkit, vrf, vrf_slave, vxcan, batadv, ifb, nlmon, vcan, team and team_slave): KC6-T01 adds 29 hsr rows, 10 accepted, 10 refused by the kernel and 9 by `ip`; KC6-T02 adds 43 netkit rows, 19 accepted, 8 refused by the kernel and 16 by `ip`; KC6-T03 adds 18 vrf, 10 vrf_slave and 18 vxcan rows, 18 accepted, 10 refused by the kernel and 18 by `ip`.

**Next action:** [KC6-T04](goip-link-kinds.md#kc6-t04-batadv-ifb-nlmon-vcan-team-and-team_slave): batadv, ifb, nlmon, vcan, team and team_slave.

## Update rules

- States are `not started`, `in progress`, `blocked`, `done` or `deferred`. There are no percentages.
- A task is `done` only when its "Done when" has passed and the evidence is in the [ledger](#verification-ledger).
- Evidence IDs (`V###` for code and VM runs, `DOC###` for docs) are immutable and append-only.
- Task IDs (`KCn-Tmm`, `KPn-Tmm`, `KGn-Tmm`) are never renumbered.
- Every task's commit flips its own checklist row and appends its `V###` rows.
- A phase's last task also closes the phase, following [Closing a phase](goip-link-kinds.md#closing-a-phase).
- A statement elsewhere in `docs/netlink/` that a task makes false is amended in that task's commit.

## Decision log

**2026-10-24:**
- **Kinds that need hardware or another creation path are out.** can, ipoib, dsa, rmnet, wwan and virt_wifi each need a lower device or hardware the VM doesn't have; netdevsim is created through sysfs, not rtnetlink. vcan, which is in, takes none of `iplink_can.c`'s options.
- **XDP is class F.** `iplink_xdp.c`'s `-d` output makes a `bpf()` syscall per program, which nlmon can't see. `iplink_xstats.c` isn't a kind; `link xstats` is a G1 read already.
- **maddress stays class F at the 7.2.0 pin.** `RTM_GETMULTICAST` reached `ip/ipmaddr.c` in `b8cdda55`, after the tag. [goip-parity-gaps.md](goip-parity-gaps.md#f-not-netlink-outside-pcap-parity) said "the 7.2 checkout" and is corrected. It is revisited at the next pin bump.
- **No fallback tunnels after late setup.** `late_setup` sets `net.core.fb_tunnels_only_for_init_net=2` before it loads the tunnel modules, so the namespaces the link-write driver builds afterwards get no `tunl0`, `gre0`, `sit0`, `ip6tnl0` or the like, and no later fixture's ifindexes move. The dump driver's tunnel set keeps its fallback devices: it loads its modules before `late_setup`, and they are half its coverage.
- **Kind modules load in `late_setup`,** not on demand through `rtnl-link-<kind>`, so the load happens at one known point after every existing row.
- **The guest kernel is 7.3.0** (`linuxPackages_latest`). The 7.3.0 dump corpus (`testdata/7_3_0/dumps`) is the reference for the dump driver's comparisons; the link-write driver is compared with a run of the code before the task, since its committed blobs are older.
- **gtp is refused by the guest kernel** (`late/kinds`: `RTNETLINK answers: Invalid argument`). `ip` 7.2.0 always asks for `IFLA_GTP_CREATE_SOCKETS`; KC4 finds the attribute the kernel wants, or moves gtp out of scope with the reason.
- **`genl/mptcp_limits_set_over` fails on kernel 7.3, before this plan.** Kernel `c8646664fbf1` ("mptcp: pm: in-kernel: increase all limits to 64") raised the limit from 8 to 64, so `ip mptcp limits set subflows 9` is accepted. The link-write driver at `34724ad7`, before KC0, fails on the same row, so it is not KC0's. It is fixed outside this plan, in the corpus row, its hermetic check and the goip and goipparity rows that copy the limit; until then the link-write driver's `NLCAP_DRIVER_FAIL` on that one row is expected.
- **An `ip` refusal before netlink exits with 1 or 255.** `invarg`, `incomplete_command` and `duparg2` call `exit(-1)`, so 255; a parse error returned up the stack instead (`parse_on_off`'s) reaches `do_cmd`, which exits with the negated return, so 1. KC1-T01's plan assumed 255 for both, and its first VM run corrected `inet_bad_on_off` to 1. Every corpus now admits both, and KG0 matches the status as well as the text.
- **bridge's `ip` refusals exit 1 where `bridge_parse_opt` returns,** as the KC1 entry above has it: an abbreviated `strcmp` keyword (`stp_m`) is an unknown word, and `parse_one_of` returns on a name it doesn't know (`stp_mode bogus`). KC2-T01's plan said 255 for both; its first run (V012) corrected them.
- **bridge's JSON keys aren't all its keywords.** `group_address` prints as `group_addr`, the `*_count` options as `*_cnt` and the `*_interval` ones as `*_intvl`; `group_fwd_mask` prints in hex. KG1's renderer follows the readbacks, not the keyword table.
- **A port's `state` reads back what the bridge makes of it.** With STP off, `br_set_port_state` sets the state sent and then runs `br_port_state_selection`, which moves an enabled port to forwarding, so `set_state`'s `state 4` reads back `forwarding`. The request pins the 4; KG1 renders what the kernel reports, as `ip` does.
- **An `ip` refusal can still send datagrams.** `backup_port` resolves its name with an `RTM_GETLINK` before `invarg` exits, so `set_backup_port_missing` has 4 datagrams at floor 0. A floor is a minimum, and the row's evidence is its `ip.err`.
- **The VM namespace's rp_filter is 2,** not the kernel's 0, so `inet_rp_filter_max` writes the value it reads back. The row still pins the request; `inet_several` (`rp_filter 1`) shows a change.
- **gtp is in scope; KC0's probe sent no option.** With nothing after `type gtp`, `iplink_parse` doesn't call `gtp_parse_opt`, no `INFO_DATA` goes out, and `gtp_validate` refuses `!data`. `role ggsn` is enough: `ip` then sends `IFLA_GTP_CREATE_SOCKETS`, and the kernel opens its own sockets. KC4-T04 has the rows; the KC0 entry above is answered.
- **7.2.0's geneve option is `local`, not `local` and `local6`.** The plan named both; `iplink_geneve.c` has one `strcmp` keyword that sends `IFLA_GENEVE_LOCAL` or `IFLA_GENEVE_LOCAL6` by the address's family, and `local6`, like `remote6`, is only an output key. KC4-T02 has a row for each family and one for the word `local6`, which `ip` refuses.
- **`get_addr` exits 1.** A bad or wrong-family address calls `exit(1)`, not `exit(-1)`, so an address `ip` can't parse reads like a returned error. KC4's tables use it; KC3's rows had no address.
- **KC5's encap rows: fou is loaded, fou6 isn't.** `late_modules` already loads `fou` for its genl family, and it registers the IPv4 FOU and GUE encap ops, so gre's, ipip's and sit's encap rows are accepted. `fou6` isn't loaded, so the ip6 kinds' `encap fou`/`gue` rows are kernel refusals (`-EINVAL`). Their requests still carry every `ENCAP_*` attribute, which is what KP and KG need; loading `fou6` would add readbacks, not request coverage.
- **The guest kernel has no `CONFIG_IPV6_SIT_6RD`.** sit's 6rd words are sent and ignored, so its 6rd rows are accepted and read back nothing; `sit/add_6rd_overlong` (48 + 24 bits, which a 6rd kernel refuses) is the canary if the config changes.
- **KC5 is five tasks, one per iproute2 parser or pair.** The drafts were argued from `link_gre.c`, `link_gre6.c`, `link_iptnl.c`, `link_ip6tnl.c`, `link_vti.c`, `link_vti6.c` and `link_xfrm.c` at `v7.2.0` and the 7.3 kernel's checks. Two findings shape every task: a bare `type <kind>` is accepted, and every parser but xfrm's fetches the device before a set and resends its current values.
- **sit's `6rd-reset` has no row: it segfaults `ip` 7.2.0.** `link_iptnl.c:288` passes the string literal `"2002::/16"` to `get_prefix`, whose `get_prefix_1` writes a NUL over the `/` (`lib/utils.c:693-695`), so `ip` faults on read-only memory before it sends anything. KC5-T03's first VM run found it (V046). KG5 decides whether goip refuses the word or sends what it was meant to, `2002::/16` with no relay prefix; parity can't compare a crash. Nothing is sent upstream.
- **A set on sit's wildcard exits 255 before netlink.** The kernel gives a sit made with no `INFO_DATA` `ttl 64` and no DF (`ipip6_netlink_parms`), the set's prefetch seeds both, and `link_iptnl.c` refuses `ttl` nonzero with `nopmtudisc`. ipip's wildcard has `ttl` 0, so its sets reach the kernel. KC5-T03 keeps the two 255 rows and reaches the kernel's point-to-point check through a `ttl inherit` wildcard.
- **KC6 is four tasks:** hsr; netkit; vrf, vrf_slave and vxcan; batadv with the five kinds `ip` has no parser for. The drafts were argued from `iplink_hsr.c`, `iplink_netkit.c`, `iplink_vrf.c`, `iplink_vxcan.c`, `iplink_batadv.c`, `iplink_ifb.c`, `iplink_nlmon.c`, `iplink_vcan.c` and `iplink_team.c` at `v7.2.0` and the 7.3 kernel's checks. Three findings shape every task:
  - `iplink.c`'s `nodev` returns rather than exits, so a set on a missing device is 1. The tunnel parsers' `exit(nodev(…))` was 255.
  - A set of only `type K` sends no `INFO_DATA` and reaches no kind code, so it is 0 for any kind.
  - A kind with no `parse_opt` takes no word after `type`. Each word is Garbage (1), or `usage()` (255) for a prefix of `help`.
- **hsr's `version` and `proto` checks in `ip` 7.2.0 test `get_u8`'s return, not the value** (`iplink_hsr.c:64-66`, `:71-73`). Any u8 passes, and the kernel refuses 2 and above. KC6-T01 pins it with `add_version_2` and `add_proto_2`; KG5 decides whether goip copies the check as written. Nothing is sent upstream.
- **netkit's rows never run under `ip -batch`.** `netkit_parse_opt` keeps its state in statics, so a second row in one batch would inherit the first's.
- **vxcan's usage goes to stdout.** `iplink_vxcan.c`'s `print_usage` ignores its `FILE *`, so `add_unknown`'s usage is in `ip.out`, not `ip.err`.
- **batadv has one attribute, not two.** The inventory said 2; `IFLA_BATADV_ALGO_NAME` is the only member after `UNSPEC`.
