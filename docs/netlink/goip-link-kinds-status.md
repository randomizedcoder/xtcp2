# goip ↔ `ip` link kinds: implementation status

**Last updated:** 2026-10-25

**Summary:**
- The design and the task plan are in [goip-link-kinds.md](goip-link-kinds.md). This document tracks progress against them.
- The plan is written (DOC001).
- KC0 is done (KC0-T01): the capture drivers load the kind modules late, after every existing row, with no fallback tunnel devices in the namespaces made afterwards, and a `kinds` sidecar records what the guest kernel creates. It creates 31 of the 32 probed kinds. gtp is refused, and KC4 decides why.
- KC1 is done (KC1-T01): 18 `ip link set dev d0 inet …` rows, 11 the kernel answers and 7 `ip` refuses before netlink, are in the rtnl corpus. They are KP0's and KG0's source.
- KC2 is done (KC2-T01, KC2-T02): a kind corpus (`kind-write-corpus.exp`) the link-write driver runs into `link-write/kinds/`, with 60 bridge rows and 42 bridge_slave rows, 80 the kernel accepts, 12 it refuses and 10 `ip` refuses before netlink. They are KP1's and KG1's source.
- KC3 is done (KC3-T01, KC3-T02, KC3-T03): 28 vlan, 34 macvlan, 6 macvtap, 18 ipvlan and 4 ipvtap rows in the kind corpus, stacked on a dummy `d0`; of the 90, 54 the kernel accepts, 14 it refuses and 22 `ip` refuses before netlink. They are KP2's and KG2's source.
- KC4 is done (KC4-T01, KC4-T02, KC4-T03, KC4-T04): KC4-T01 adds 73 vxlan rows, 46 the kernel accepts, 12 it refuses and 15 `ip` refuses before netlink; KC4-T02 adds 49 geneve rows, 22 accepted, 13 refused by the kernel and 14 by `ip`; KC4-T03 adds 16 bareudp and 20 amt rows, 11 accepted, 12 refused by the kernel and 13 by `ip`; KC4-T04 adds 19 gtp rows, 7 accepted, 4 refused by the kernel and 8 by `ip`. They are KP3's and KG3's source.
- KC5 is done (KC5-T01, KC5-T02, KC5-T03, KC5-T04, KC5-T05; planned in DOC006): KC5-T01 adds 45 gre, 11 gretap and 25 erspan rows, 44 the kernel accepts, 16 it refuses and 21 `ip` refuses before netlink; KC5-T02 adds 36 ip6gre, 9 ip6gretap and 19 ip6erspan rows, 36 accepted, 14 refused by the kernel and 14 by `ip`; KC5-T03 adds 47 ipip and 37 sit rows, 46 accepted, 14 refused by the kernel and 24 by `ip` (`6rd-reset` has no row: it segfaults `ip`); KC5-T04 adds 49 ip6tnl rows, 29 accepted, 7 refused by the kernel and 13 by `ip`; KC5-T05 adds 23 vti, 12 vti6 and 23 xfrm rows, 27 accepted, 15 refused by the kernel and 16 by `ip`. They are KP4's and KG4's source.
- KC6 is done (KC6-T01, KC6-T02, KC6-T03, KC6-T04; planned in DOC007): KC6-T01 adds 29 hsr rows, 10 accepted, 10 refused by the kernel and 9 by `ip`; KC6-T02 adds 43 netkit rows, 19 accepted, 8 refused by the kernel and 16 by `ip`; KC6-T03 adds 18 vrf, 10 vrf_slave and 18 vxcan rows, 18 accepted, 10 refused by the kernel and 18 by `ip`; KC6-T04 adds 17 batadv, 9 ifb, 6 nlmon, 13 vcan, 14 team and 4 team_slave rows, 29 accepted, 18 refused by the kernel and 16 by `ip`. They are KP5's and KG5's source.
- KC7 is done (KC7-T01, KC7-T02; planned in DOC008): KC7-T01 commits the 7.3.0 link-write corpus, `testdata/7_3_0/link-write`, 10558 files from one run of the runner at the 7.2.0 pin, every kind row with its status, request and readbacks. KC7-T02 is the gate, `TestLinkKindUniverse`: of the 46 kinds 7.2.0's `iplink_*.c` and `link_*.c` name, 39 are captured with an accepted row and a `-d -j` readback, and 7 are excluded with a reason.
- The KC tier is done.
- KP0 is done (KP0-T01; planned in DOC009): `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_inet_devconf.go` encodes the `inet` write's `IFLA_AF_SPEC` byte for byte as `ip` does for the 11 KC1 rows the kernel answered, decodes it back, and decodes the dump's 33-value devconf array. KP1 is planned (DOC010).
- KP1 is done (KP1-T01, KP1-T02; planned in DOC010): `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_bridge.go` is the bridge's 53-row option table and `xtcpnl_bridge_slave.go` the port's 44-row one. Each has a decoder that renders every accepted row's dump exactly as its `-d -j` readback prints it (50 bridge rows, 30 port rows), and an encoder that rebuilds the data nest of every row that sent a request byte for byte (55 and 37). `LinkInfo` carries `Bridge` and `BridgeSlave`. KP2 is planned (DOC011).
- KP2 is done (KP2-T01, KP2-T02, KP2-T03; planned in DOC011): `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_vlan.go` decodes the vlan kind into `LinkInfo.Vlan`, rendering all 15 accepted rows' dumps as their `-d -j` readbacks print them, and encodes the `IFLA_INFO_DATA` of the 21 rows that sent a request byte for byte. `xtcpnl_macvlan.go` does the same for macvlan and macvtap into `LinkInfo.Macvlan` (25 accepted rows, 30 requests), and `xtcpnl_ipvlan.go` for ipvlan and ipvtap into `LinkInfo.Ipvlan` (14 and 17). KP3 is planned (DOC012).
- KP3 is done (KP3-T01, KP3-T02, KP3-T03, KP3-T04; planned in DOC012): `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_vxlan.go` decodes the vxlan kind into `LinkInfo.Vxlan`, rendering all 46 accepted rows' dumps as their `-d -j` readbacks print them, and encodes the `IFLA_INFO_DATA` of the 58 rows that sent a request byte for byte, adds and sets alike. `xtcpnl_geneve.go` does the same for geneve into `LinkInfo.Geneve` (22 accepted rows, 35 requests), `xtcpnl_bareudp.go` for bareudp into `LinkInfo.Bareudp` (6 and 10), `xtcpnl_amt.go` for amt into `LinkInfo.Amt` (5 and 13) and `xtcpnl_gtp.go` for gtp into `LinkInfo.Gtp` (7 and 11); they share `xtcpnl_kind_wire.go`. KP4 is planned (DOC013).
- KP4 is done (KP4-T01, KP4-T02, KP4-T03, KP4-T04, KP4-T05; planned in DOC013): `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_gre.go` decodes the six gre kinds into `LinkInfo.Gre`, rendering all 44 accepted gre, gretap and erspan rows' dumps as their `-d -j` readbacks print them, and encodes the `IFLA_INFO_DATA` of the 60 rows that sent a request byte for byte, sets seeded from their prefetch. `xtcpnl_gre6.go` does the same for ip6gre, ip6gretap and ip6erspan (36 accepted rows, 50 requests), and `xtcpnl_iptun.go` for ipip and sit into `LinkInfo.Iptun` (46 and 60), which ip6tnl's dump also fills; `xtcpnl_ip6tnl.go` encodes ip6tnl (29 accepted rows, 36 requests). `xtcpnl_vti.go` does the same for vti and vti6 into `LinkInfo.Vti` (18 and 25), and `xtcpnl_xfrm_link.go` for xfrm into `LinkInfo.Xfrm` (9 and 17). The shared `TunnelEncap` block lives in `xtcpnl_kind_wire.go`. KP5 is planned (DOC014).
- KP5 is done (KP5-T01, KP5-T02, KP5-T03, KP5-T04; planned in DOC014): `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_hsr.go` decodes hsr into `LinkInfo.Hsr`, rendering all 10 accepted rows' dumps as their `-d -j` readbacks print them, and encodes the `IFLA_INFO_DATA` of the 20 rows that sent a request byte for byte. `xtcpnl_netkit.go` does the same for netkit into `LinkInfo.Netkit` (19 accepted rows, 27 requests), its `PEER_INFO` nest shared with `EncodeVethPeer` through `putPeerNest`. `xtcpnl_vrf.go` encodes vrf's 12 requests (the 8 accepted rows' dumps and the 3 enslaved vrf_slave rows' render through the existing `vrfTableAttr`), and `xtcpnl_vxcan.go` vxcan's 9 through `putPeerNest`; no vrf_slave request carries `INFO_SLAVE_DATA`. `xtcpnl_batadv.go` encodes batadv's 12 requests; batadv has no `fill_info`, and ifb, nlmon, vcan, team and team_slave send and dump no kind data at all, which `TestKindsWithoutData` holds for all 46 of their requests.

## Update rules

- States are `not started`, `in progress`, `blocked`, `done` or `deferred`. There are no percentages.
- A task is `done` only when its "Done when" has passed and the evidence is in the [ledger](#verification-ledger).
- Evidence IDs (`V###` for code and VM runs, `DOC###` for docs) are immutable and append-only.
- Task IDs (`KCn-Tmm`, `KPn-Tmm`, `KGn-Tmm`) are never renumbered.
- Every task's commit flips its own checklist row and appends its `V###` rows.
- A phase's last task also closes the phase, following [Closing a phase](goip-link-kinds.md#closing-a-phase).
- A statement elsewhere in `docs/netlink/` that a task makes false is amended in that task's commit.
