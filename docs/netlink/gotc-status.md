# gotc (tc qdisc, class, filter, flower and chain show): implementation status

**Last updated:** 2026-11-24

**Summary:**
- The design and task plan are in [gotc.md](gotc.md); this document tracks progress
  against them. The ledger starts at **V001 / DOC001**.
- **9S (skeleton): done.** `gotc` (`cmd/gotc`, `internal/gotc`, the nix package `gotc`)
  runs tc.c's main: the option loop in its `matches()` order, `do_cmd`'s object dispatch,
  `do_qdisc`'s and `do_class`'s verbs, usage, `-batch` (file or stdin, `-force`) and
  `-netns`. `/proc/net/psched` (`tc_core_init`) and the `-nm`/`-cf` class names databases
  (`cls_names_init`) are read through a `sysFS` root, so tests and replays are hermetic.
  `qdisc show` and `class show` return not-implemented until 9R; filter, chain, actions,
  monitor, exec and every write are refused (V001).
- **9C (captures): done.** Two spaces, `tcqdisc` (142 rows) and `tcclass` (82), over
  nine fixtures, every positive plain and `-j`. The corpus is in
  `pkg/xtcpnl/testdata/7_3_1/ws/tc{qdisc,class}/`. Two batches on one boot agree, apart
  from two named exceptions. The capture found Q34: a second `-g` line in a batch aborts
  tc with a double free (V002).
- **9P (parsers): done.** `pkg/xtcpnl` decodes each in-slice kind's `TCA_OPTIONS` and
  xstats as its `tc/q_*.c` printer reads them (`ParseTcQopt`, `ParseTcXstats`), and
  `TCA_STATS2`/`TCA_STATS` as `print_tcstats*_attr` does (`ParseTcStats2`,
  `DecodeTcStats`). `BuildTcDumpRequest` builds tc's qdisc and class dumps byte for byte.
  `FamilyHdrLen` now models tcmsg for the qdisc and class messages (V003).
- **9R (render): done.** `tc qdisc show` and `tc class show` print every slice kind, as
  text and JSON, with `-s -d -r -p -o -g -iec -nm`, alone or in a batch. All 224 capture
  rows replay byte for byte, apart from one named exception and the two write rows gotc
  refuses. 216 tdc readbacks render exactly; the 245 that hold an out-of-slice kind are
  refused by name, and their counts are pinned (V004).
- **9B (bug hunt): done.** [upstream/iproute2-tc-qdisc-class.md](upstream/iproute2-tc-qdisc-class.md) records 33
  findings and one kernel note. Not submitted to netdev. The new ones are Q36 (`-g` reads freed nodes on every nested
  tree, confirmed byte for byte by the corpus) and Q35 (`parse_rtattr_nested_compat` trusts the inner `rta_len`). Q14
  is fixed at HEAD by `9dd1bec6b863`. Q10, Q23 and Q32 were dropped (DOC001).
- **9L (live diff): done.** On the workbench VM, gotc and tc 7.2.0 ran every one of the 224 capture rows live, over
  the 24 fixtures. 221 were identical. The 3 that differ are the expected ones: the two refused writes, and Q34's
  heap-garbage stderr line (V005).
- **Increment 10 (filter and chain show/get).** The plan: `tc filter show/get` and `tc chain show/get` for u32,
  fw, route, basic, cgroup, flow and matchall, with the cmp, u32, canid, meta and nbyte ematches, the action
  frame and gact. Flower is increment 11; the other actions, police, `monitor` and `actions list` are 12.
- **10C (captures): done.** One space, `tcfilter` (159 rows), over six fixtures, every positive plain and `-j`.
  The corpus is in `pkg/xtcpnl/testdata/7_3_1/ws/tcfilter/`. Two batches on one boot agree on stdout, stderr and
  rc; the replies differ only in the actions' `tcf_t` timers. The capture confirmed 22 of the plan's leads. It
  also found that `-br` fails with EMSGSIZE rather than EOPNOTSUPP (F21), and that no cgroup filter can be
  got (V006).
- **10P (parsers): done.** `pkg/xtcpnl` decodes the seven kinds' `TCA_OPTIONS` (`ParseTcFopt`), ematch trees and
  the cmp, u32, nbyte, canid and meta payloads, and action tables with gact, each as its tc printer reads it.
  `BuildTcFilterDump` and `BuildTcFilterGet` build tc's filter and chain requests byte for byte. `FamilyHdrLen` now
  models tcmsg for the filter and chain messages (V007).
- **10R (render): done.** gotc now prints `tc filter` and `tc chain` show and get for the seven kinds, with their
  ematches and gact actions, as text and JSON, alone or in a batch. All 159 tcfilter rows replay byte for byte,
  apart from the four that are refused by name and the one write. 254 tdc filter and chain readbacks render
  exactly (470 with qdisc and class), and the 32 that hold bpf or a non-gact action are refused, with their counts
  pinned (V008).
- **10B (bug write-up): done.** [upstream/iproute2-tc-filter.md](upstream/iproute2-tc-filter.md) records 39
  findings and four kernel notes. Not submitted to netdev. The new ones are F25 (`parse_ematch` undoes a look-ahead
  the parser never made) and F26 (`rtnl_tab_initialize` writes `tab[256]`). F21 holds, but with EMSGSIZE (K3). No
  lead was dropped (DOC002).
- **10L (live diff): done.** On the workbench VM, gotc and tc 7.2.0 ran all 159 tcfilter rows live. 154 were
  identical. The 5 that differ are the expected ones: four refusals by name (police, mirred) and the refused write
  (V009).
- **Increment 11 (flower).** The plan: every key tc 7.2.0's `flower_print_opt` prints, including the enc_* keys
  and the geneve, vxlan, erspan, gtp and pfcp options, plus `-br`. Actions stay at the frame plus gact.
- **11C (captures): done.** One space, `tcflower` (97 rows), over five fixtures, every positive plain and `-j`. The
  corpus is in `pkg/xtcpnl/testdata/7_3_1/ws/tcflower/`. Two batches on one boot agree on stderr and rc, and on
  stdout apart from gact's `-s` seconds; the replies differ only in the actions' `tcf_t` timers. The capture
  confirmed FL1 (no gtp in JSON), FL2 (pfcp's seid cut to 32 bits), FL3, FL4, FL6, FL7 and FL16. It found FL21
  (`parse_fopt` gets get's argc by value, so any kind options on a `get` end in "Garbage", rc 255) and FL22 (a
  block `get` must name a parent the kernel ignores). FL20 is not what the plan said: a filter's terse dump carries
  no action index, so `-br` prints just `gact ` (V010).
- **11P (parsers): done.** `pkg/xtcpnl/xtcpnl_tc_flower.go` has the 111 `TCA_FLOWER_*` types and the nested
  enums, flower in `ParseTcFopt`, and decoders for each nest the printer opens itself (geneve's walk as C steps it,
  vxlan, erspan, gtp, pfcp, the MPLS LSE list, cfm). It also has `ParseAttrTableLoose`, C's walk with its
  `!!!Deficit`, and `TcFlowerGetOpts`, whose bytes match the captured get requests. Every flower filter in the
  corpus decodes with no deficit (V011).
- **11R (gotc flower): done.** `internal/gotc/f_flower.go` is `flower_print_opt` with every helper, the leads kept
  as tc prints them, and `addr.go` has the address and masked-value printers (glibc's `inet_ntop` forms included).
  `filter get ... flower` sends tc's bytes. All 97 `tcflower` rows replay byte for byte, beside the 383 rows of
  increments 9 and 10. A match key on a get is refused by name, as is any attribute tc reads without checking
  (V012).

## Findings (9B write-up: [upstream/iproute2-tc-qdisc-class.md](upstream/iproute2-tc-qdisc-class.md))

- **Q24 confirmed in code:** `-c` matches `-cf` before `-color`, so `tc -c qdisc show`
  takes `qdisc` as the names file and fails on the object `show`.
- **Q33 (new lead):** `cls_names_init` (tc/tc_util.c:34-60) loads the `-cf` file, then
  `CONF_ETC_DIR/tc_cls`, else `CONF_USR_DIR/tc_cls`. When that last load fails it frees
  the whole database, so the `-cf` file's names are dropped too and `-nm` prints numbers,
  with exit 0. Neither default exists in the workbench, so `-nm -cf FILE` never names.
- **Order quirk:** `do_qdisc` tests `link` (a write) before `list`, so `tc qdisc l` and
  `tc qdisc li` are writes; `tc class l` is a list, since class has no `link`.
- **Q34 (new, found by 9C):** `tc -g -batch` with two `class show` lines aborts (rc 134).
  `graph_cls_show` (tc_class.c) frees each node it prints but never resets the static list
  heads, so the second line's graph walks freed nodes ("!!!Deficit ..., rta_len=<garbage>",
  then "free(): double free detected in tcache 2"). Text output is lost, since stdout is a
  pipe and abort never flushes it.
- **Batch order:** `batch()` dumps the links (`ll_init_map`) before `do_batch` opens the
  file, so a missing batch file still costs a link dump.
- **Compat nest overread (new lead, from 9P):** `__parse_rtattr_nested_compat`
  (lib/libnetlink.c:1552) treats the bytes after the struct as one rtattr and parses its
  `RTA_PAYLOAD` without checking `rta_len` against what remains. A prio `TCA_OPTIONS` whose
  inner `rta_len` overruns makes tc read past the attribute. The kernel never sends such a
  nest, so only a crafted message reaches it. gotc refuses it (`ErrTcCompatNest`).
- **Graph walks freed nodes even on one line (new, from 9R):** `graph_cls_show` frees each
  class once printed, but a parent's later `graph_indent` follows `right_node` into the
  freed child and reads its `hlist.next`. glibc has overwritten that word with its free
  list pointer (never NULL), so the tree's closing indent prints `|    ` where
  `     ` was meant. The corpus graphs agree with that reading. 9B will confirm it with a
  deeper tree and record it as a lead.
